How a cake gets made

Four steps, one conversation. Most orders are agreed inside a single thread of messages.

01

Tell us the occasion

The date, roughly how many people, and the feeling you're after. A screenshot of something you love is the fastest possible brief.

02

We design it

We come back with a direction — shape, palette, finish, flavour — and a price. Nothing is baked until you've said yes to the design.

03

It's made by hand

Baked to order in small batches, then finished by hand. Sugar work, painting and detailing are done last so they arrive sharp.

04

Cake@Venue

Collect it, or let us bring it to the venue and set it on the table so nobody has to carry a tiered cake across a car park.

Choose the inside
